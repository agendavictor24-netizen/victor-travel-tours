Who We Are

Victor Travel & Tours is a professional travel company based in Kampala, Uganda. We specialize in creating unforgettable travel experiences for local and international travelers seeking adventure, relaxation, and cultural discovery.

From wildlife safaris and gorilla trekking to holiday packages and corporate travel solutions, we help travelers discover the beauty of Uganda and Africa with confidence.

To provide exceptional travel experiences through professional service, safety, affordability, and customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become one of East Africa's leading travel and tourism companies known for excellence and unforgettable adventures.
